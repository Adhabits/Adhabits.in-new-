Why most affiliate marketers stall below ₹1L a month.

You've made sales. You know your product. But every month starts the same way: waiting on a referral, posting a reel, hoping someone reaches out. That's rarely an effort problem. It's a system problem.

01No predictable lead flow.

Referrals and reels work until they don't. You can't scale something you can't control.

02An offer that doesn't convert cold traffic.

What works when someone already trusts you falls apart on a stranger's first scroll.

03Messaging that's too broad.

Generic copy gets views. Specific copy gets buyers. Most affiliates never learn the difference.

04A funnel built for the wrong audience.

Leads come in but don't convert, because the funnel doesn't fit your price point. The leads aren't the problem.

05Follow-up that leaks.

Slow replies, no pre-framing, no nurture. Qualified leads go cold before they ever reach a call.

How it works

One broken step breaks everything. So we fix all five.

01
STEP 1

Offer positioning for cold traffic

We rebuild your offer for strangers. Not what you sell, but how it's positioned: the specific promise that makes someone scrolling at 11pm stop and think "this is for me."

02
STEP 2

Ad creatives that pre-qualify intent

Messaging specific enough that your ideal client feels spoken to, and everyone else scrolls past. That precision keeps cost per lead low and lead quality high.

03
STEP 3

Funnel architecture that converts

Quick-form, WhatsApp VSL, profile-to-DM or a multi-step application funnel. We pick the one that fits your price point and audience, then build and launch it.

04
STEP 4

Lead qualification & nurture

Pre-call sequences that warm prospects up before they speak to you, and filters that remove time-wasters before they ever reach your calendar.

05
STEP 5

Conversion system & scale

Once your funnel runs at a profitable cost per lead, we scale with budget protocols, creative refresh cycles and retargeting built to hold ROAS as spend grows.
